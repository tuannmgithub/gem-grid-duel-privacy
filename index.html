Privacy Policy

Gem Grid (Android app com.gemgrid.duel)
Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gem Grid ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Gem Grid is a free, single-player gem block-puzzle game supported by advertising. From version 1.0.19 the game runs entirely on your device: there is no game account and no game server.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, gem skins, records and settings) is stored on your device and is not sent to us. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup (controlled by your Google account, not by us) so it can be restored on a new device. The following data is collected by the third-party services the app uses:

DataPurpose
App usage events (screens opened, levels played, features used) and an app-instance identifierAnalytics — understanding how the game is played so we can improve it (Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config)
Crash and diagnostic data (device model, OS version, crash traces)Fixing bugs and stability issues (Google Firebase Crashlytics)
Advertising ID and ad interaction dataServing and measuring in-app ads (Google AdMob)

To deliver new and rebalanced levels without an app update, the app may download a level file from our public web page (GitHub Pages, tuannmgithub.github.io). This request contains no personal or game data; like any web request, the hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request information.

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, "your daily gift is ready") are scheduled and shown locally by your device; no data is sent to us for this.

2. How We Use Information

  • Analyze aggregate usage to improve game design, difficulty and balance.
  • Detect and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • Show advertising that funds the free game, including optional rewarded ads.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps

4. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward) and interstitial ads between levels. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, which deletes it permanently. Analytics and crash data are retained according to the retention settings of Google Firebase.

Earlier versions (before 1.0.19) used an online game account identified by a device-generated ID. That online service is being retired; its account data will be deleted when the servers are shut down. You may also request earlier deletion by contacting us at the email address below.

6. Children

Gem Grid is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We never receive your game progress (it stays on your device and, if you enabled it, in your own Android/Google backup). Data handled by Firebase and AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/GDPR, California/CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
